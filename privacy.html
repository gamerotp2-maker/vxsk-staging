PLAYER INFORMATION / PRIVACY

PRIVACY NOTICE.

VXSK is an annual gaming event created by Voxzan and Shark. This notice describes the website's player application process. Contact gamerotp2@gmail.com with questions or to request deletion.

Information you provide

When you apply, you can provide your selected game and mode, in-game username, platform, region, optional teammate usernames, a preferred contact method (Discord, email, phone, or in-game username), and an optional message. Please do not submit addresses, passwords, identity documents, or other sensitive information.

Why information is collected

We use application details only to review interest, organize VXSK events, and contact applicants about VXSK. Applications are not published by this website. Submitting an application is not a confirmed event entry.

Find an opponent requests

The optional match-finder form collects your game, mode, rank, gamertag, region, available times and private contact handle so VXSK staff can arrange a match. New requests are sent to a private Supabase table that staff can review. They are not automatically published or displayed to other players. Players can view their own request status and delete their own requests; authorized staff can review and delete requests. Do not put phone numbers, home addresses or sensitive details in gamertags or free-text fields.

Player accounts

If you create an account, Supabase Auth processes account email addresses, password credentials, verification, sign-in sessions, optional two-step verification, and password recovery. Uploaded avatar images are stored in private Supabase Storage. Once the account email is confirmed, your chosen VXSK nickname is automatically searchable by other signed-in, unblocked accounts, and your uploaded avatar is shared through short-lived authenticated image links. This is part of the staging community experience; it cannot be disabled independently. You may still control whether accepted friends see you as Online or Offline, and you may block individual accounts. VXSK does not store passwords in its HTML or registration forms. A verified player account and sign-in are required for tournament applications. You can request account deletion from the VXSK contact email; the site owner must handle deletion in the Supabase Auth dashboard and follow the provider's policies. We do not publish member email addresses.

Community features (staging only)

Confirmed VXSK accounts appear automatically in signed-in users' nickname search, with their chosen nickname and uploaded avatar, except where either person has blocked the other. Email addresses, phone numbers, application details and contact information are not shown. You can choose whether accepted friends see an approximate Online/Offline indicator based on recent website activity; Offline does not hide your search result, nickname or avatar. Blocking a player hides each of you from the other and prevents new friend requests. Previously issued short-lived avatar links may stay usable until expiration. Players can send friend requests, accept, reject or block others. Friends can send optional showmatch challenges, including their chosen game and mode; an opponent must explicitly accept before an accepted challenge is shown to authorized staff for event scheduling. Only mutually accepted friends can send private messages, which are stored in Supabase for delivery and moderation. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Reports of messages can be retained for review by the VXSK team; reports are not publicly displayed. Do not share personal contact information, passwords or verification codes in messages. These social tools are undergoing staging tests and are not yet approved for the official website. VXSK must define message retention periods and a moderation process before public launch. You may contact the VXSK team to request deletion or report abuse.

Private squad group chats (staging only)

Signed-in players may invite up to seven accepted, unblocked friends into private text groups. An invitation does not give access to group messages until the invited player accepts. Group messages, group names, memberships, and submitted reports are stored in Supabase and can be accessed only through group-specific authenticated database functions. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Group members can leave at any time; the creator may delete the entire group and its messages by leaving. Group membership reveals participating nicknames to other accepted members. Sending and invitations are rate-limited. A block hides the blocked person's messages from you, but does not erase messages or automatically remove that member from every existing group. Group reports are saved, but the staff review interface is not yet built; do not launch group chats publicly until reporting, moderation and retention controls are tested. Group members can explicitly join experimental two-to-four-person WebRTC audio/video calls; the browser requests microphone/camera permission only when joining. Optional screen and tab-audio sharing require separate permission. Media is peer-to-peer and is not recorded by VXSK; temporary peer signaling and session metadata are stored privately in Supabase. Fullscreen viewing does not authorize screen sharing from a device whose browser lacks capture support. Do not share passwords, authentication codes, addresses or sensitive personal details in group chats.

Friend audio/video calls (staging only)

Call invitations are available only between accepted friends who have not blocked each other. An incoming call must be accepted before any camera or microphone access is requested. The browser transfers audio and video using WebRTC; VXSK does not record or store call media. Supabase temporarily holds call invitations and technical connection data (SDP and ICE candidates, which may include network information). Connection details are restricted to participants and cleared at call end; brief call metadata may remain for anti-spam rate limiting and staging troubleshooting. To discover peer-to-peer routes, the current preview uses an external STUN server; network addresses may be exposed to that service and to a call participant. WebRTC can fail on restrictive networks until a TURN relay is configured. The close (X) control minimizes an ongoing call to a small card; End Call actually disconnects. Incoming-call ringtones use a local audio file rather than a microphone. The ringtone can be switched off; browsers may require a user click before sound is allowed. Speaking-ring indicators analyze live sound levels in your browser without recording or uploading additional voice data. You can end a call, mute your microphone, disable your camera, request fullscreen, share a chosen screen or tab, or block another user. Shared-screen audio is requested only after you click Share Screen and may not be available in every browser or for every shared surface; the browser may offer a separate Share tab audio checkbox. When present, shared audio is sent alongside (not in place of) your microphone. Only the call participant receives it. Please report harassment or unwanted contact to the VXSK support email. Call features remain testing-only until two-account privacy and safety checks pass.

Arena Hub — tournaments, teams and badges (staging only)

The optional Arena Hub may store published tournament and calendar details, public team names and descriptions, captain/member account IDs, voluntary team join requests, and assigned match check-ins. Teams are public only when published by their captain; a displayed roster can show a confirmed player's searchable nickname. Team captains can review and decide membership requests. Your gaming activity status is hidden by default and only visible to other signed-in players if you turn it on. Personal win/loss statistics derive from explicitly assigned match results. Badges and assignment notifications appear privately to their recipient. General abuse reports are private to the reporter and verified staff. Reactions on direct messages are visible only to the two message participants; messages remain subject to existing friendship and blocking rules. Staging features are not active on the official production website. You may request review or deletion using the contact above; retention and moderation processes must be finalized before public launch.

Website performance and offline files

Optional Low-Lag Mode stores a preference in your browser and disables decorative effects; it does not improve your in-game ping or alter microphone and call video tracks. On supported browsers, VXSK can be installed to your home screen and a service worker may cache public website code, icons, and images so repeat visits load faster. It does not intentionally cache Supabase authentication or private chat API responses. Sign out and clear browser/site data on shared devices if desired.

Account switching

Switch Account signs out the current VXSK session and asks you to sign in to another account; every account must pass its own authentication and 2FA. As an optional convenience, the site can remember an account nickname and email address in this browser only when you explicitly choose to save them. No account passwords, authenticator codes, or multiple active sessions are stored as account shortcuts. On a shared device, leave this option off and clear saved account shortcuts when finished.

Where submissions go

Tournament applications are submitted through the logged-in player session directly to Supabase, where the verified account email and unique account ID are stored with the application for staff review. The Find Rival form sends requests to a separate private Supabase staff inbox. Existing older Netlify test forms, if any, are not automatically imported into Supabase. VXSK uses these details only for event administration. Staff decisions can be checked by signing in and opening My Applications. Automatic approval/rejection emails are currently paused; this does not affect separate authentication emails used for account verification or password recovery.

Retention and your choices

We aim to retain applications only for as long as they are relevant to organizing VXSK and to delete them when no longer needed. You may request access to or deletion of application details at gamerotp2@gmail.com. Before launch, the event operator should define and publish a specific retention period and review applicable privacy rules.

External media and links

This site links to YouTube, Kick, Discord, Instagram, and TikTok. YouTube thumbnails load from YouTube's thumbnail delivery service. Following external links takes you to those platforms, which have their own privacy rules.
