ENDLESS STORIES.

What is VXSK? VXSK is an annual gaming event founded by Voxzan and Shark. It brings players, creators and communities together through competitive showmatches, unforgettable clips and the thrill of a shared stage.

From first-time challengers to established competitors, VXSK gives every player a chance to become part of the story. Expect organized matchups, livestream moments, friendly rivalries and events shaped by the community.

Our arena starts with Rocket League, with VALORANT and League of Legends on the horizon.
